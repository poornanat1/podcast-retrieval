# Licensing and data responsibilities

Publicly accessible podcast content is not automatically unrestricted. Audio, artwork, descriptions, and transcripts delivered through public RSS feeds can remain copyrighted. These rules bind every component in this repository:

- Follow the terms of discovery providers (currently the Particle data platform) and of each publisher.
- Preserve publisher attribution and canonical links in the catalog and in any UI.
- Do not scrape closed platforms or download restricted content; audio is never downloaded — only enclosure URLs are stored.
- Store only the metadata and artifacts the project requires.
- Respect feed removal and correction requests: removal deletes the derived records and embeddings, not just the feed row.
- Review commercial rights before retaining or transforming transcripts.
- Voice recordings are temporary by default and deleted after transcription unless the user explicitly consents to retention. Voice, search, and playback data must have export and deletion controls.
